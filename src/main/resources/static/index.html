START WITH THE PEOPLE

Two sellers. One buyer. A fair set of rules.

Both sellers want the same price. Our buyer wants 100 shares. Who should sell first, and should the buyer pay their maximum? Let the engine show you.

This creates a separate practice market using real orders in the local engine. It does not place orders on a stock exchange.

The rule is simple. Best price first. At the same price, whoever arrived first. The trade happens at the waiting seller's price.

Three trading words, in plain English.

Order: a request to buy or sell a quantity of shares.

Order book: the list of buyers and sellers still waiting to trade.

Limit price: the most a buyer will pay, or the least a seller will accept.

THE MATCHING RULES

Every fill has a reason.

  1. Better price goes first.

    Buyers prefer the lowest ask. Sellers prefer the highest bid.

  2. At the same price, earlier goes first.

    The engine uses an ordered sequence to keep arrival priority deterministic.

  3. The resting price wins.

    A buy limit of 150.15 meeting a resting sell at 150.10 executes at 150.10.

  4. Only limit orders can rest.

    A market order fills against available liquidity and cancels any remainder.

The guided demo writes to a new sandbox symbol so you can observe these rules without changing another instrument's book.
